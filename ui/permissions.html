📁 SI Folders – site access

Firefox needs your permission before SI Folders can show its panel on these sites. Without access, the 📁 button does not appear on that site. On x.com / twitter.com the panel only appears on Grok (/i/grok); the rest of X is left alone.

After granting, reload open AI tabs. You can also manage access in about:addons → SI Folders → Permissions, or click the SI Folders toolbar button.
